  Frequency Shift Keying (FSK)
  Frequency of the carrier signal is varied to represent binary 1
  or 0. Amplitude and Phase remains constant.
  The simplest is Binary Frequency Shift Keying (BFSK)

=== SLIDE 29 ===
  Phase Shift Keying (PSK)
  Phase of the carrier signal is varied to represent binary 1 or 0.
  Amplitude and Frequency remains constant.
  The simplest is Binary PSK
