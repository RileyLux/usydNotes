## London Post Office Scandal
- Between 1999 - 2015 Uk post office used faulty system
- Prosecuted 900+ ppl based on incorrect numbers
- Regardless of numerous reports and complaints, they dismissed the claims and didnt investigate the system

## Software Engineering
A discipline of engineering that is concerned with all aspects of a software production
- Programming and coding
- Methods and tools for production
- Technical processes, project management and development tools
- Could involve hardware (Systems engineer)

## Examples of Failures
### ASX CHESS replacement collapse
- Team locked in specific deadline without understanding true scope
- Kept adding more features and requirements without adjusting timeline
- Testing kept getting pushed out
- Last stages of process were rushed

### VW Software
- VW wanted a base software platform for all its brands
- Tried to satisfy many sub-brands requirements simultaneously 
- Couldn't align each brands needs to each other


## Agile process
- Less focus on documentation and more on development and testing
- Flexible during development
- Deliver products earlier
- Collaboration over strict processes
